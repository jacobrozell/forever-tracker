Gold & mail

House rules for making money while everyone is broke.

Rules

  1. Mail full stacks only.Postage is 30 copper per attached stack, however many items are in it.
  2. Mail straight to the crafter.Stopping at Redstone first doubles the postage.
  3. Sell from the gatherer.Post raw mats on the Auction House from whoever picked them. No postage at all.
  4. Never vendor crafted gear.Vendor prices were cut in October. Sell to players or disenchant with Fatal.
  5. Sell common mats early, hold Scarce ones.Linen, copper and peacebloom flood the market. Pristine Leather and other Scarce mats climb.
  6. Craft what players buy.Rum's dyes and Sulfuric Acid go into lots of recipes. Corwyn's bags always sell.
  7. Turn in Waylaid Crates.Fill them with spare mats for Merchant's Favor, or sell them empty.
